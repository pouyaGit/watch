## 3. Why the hourly trigger remains

`systemd/watch-research.timer` stays `OnCalendar=hourly` (verified:
`systemd-analyze calendar hourly` → `*-*-* *:00:00`). The timer is merely a
trigger; the application window is authoritative:

- hourly firing gives ~12 opportunities to run inside the 12:00–00:00 window
  instead of depending on one exact daily trigger;
- outside 12:00–00:00 the service exits without research work
  (`skipped=outside_window`, covered by `test_outside_window_does_nothing`);
- no single-daily-trigger replacement was made — there is no concrete reason
  to rely on one exact firing.
